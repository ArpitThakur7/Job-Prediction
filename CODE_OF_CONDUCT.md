# Code of conduct

Fletch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and don't harass anyone.
Many people here are job hunting, which is stressful. Be kind.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ArpitThakur7). Reports are handled confidentially.
